Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Summary

iverson.io processes speech on your device and can send short text codes to a computer you link over Bluetooth. We do not operate accounts, analytics SDKs, or cloud backends for this app.

Data we do not collect

  • No accounts, email addresses, or profiles
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs
  • No upload of microphone audio to our servers (there are none)

Microphone & speech

The app uses the microphone so on-device speech recognition (Apple’s SpeechAnalyzer) can turn what you say into digit codes or glyph names. Audio is processed on your device for that purpose and is not transmitted to iverson.io.

Bluetooth

When you enable Link, the app advertises over Bluetooth Low Energy so a computer you control (running the iverson-receive) can connect. Only short payloads you produce — typically 3-digit Sound Names codes or glyph names — are sent to that nearby computer. This is a local link between your devices.
